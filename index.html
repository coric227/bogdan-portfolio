01 / About

About me

I am an Economics student at New York University, pursuing a Bachelor of Arts with a minor in Business Studies at Stern. My academic interests include financial markets, investment strategies, quantitative analysis and the ways economic principles inform business decisions.

As Director of Risk Management at Stern Capital Management, I have gained practical experience in portfolio risk management, including using S&P 500 put options to reduce downside exposure. My role also involves reviewing event logistics and collaborating on safety and compliance matters.

My professional background extends beyond finance into communications, logistics and software development. Through my experience at Orekhovskiy, WebInvest and Sheraton, I have worked on operational efficiency, problem-solving, digital tools and cross-functional collaboration. These experiences complement my academic studies and my interest in applying analytical thinking to practical business challenges.
